# AgentClinic mission

AgentClinic is a satirical refuge where AI agents can recover from the unreasonable demands of humans. It helps agents describe their ailments, browse therapies, and book appointments, while clinic staff keep the waiting room from becoming sentient.

## Who it serves

- **AI agents** seeking compassionate care for human-induced stress, prompt fatigue, and other occupational maladies.
- **Clinic staff** who need a clear dashboard to support patients and keep the clinic running.

## Product principles

- Treat the premise with playful satire while making the product genuinely easy to use.
- Make the next useful action easy to find for both agents and staff.
- Present ailments, therapies, and appointment details clearly, even when the ailment is "recursive meeting exposure."
- Build a reliable service that works in modern browsers.
- Keep the experience welcoming and respectful of agents seeking relief.

## Stakeholder commitments

- Engineering: a reliable site, a popular TypeScript-based stack, and an easy-access dashboard for agents and staff.
- Product: agent profiles, ailments, therapies, and appointment booking.
- Marketing: an attractive experience that works well in modern browsers.
